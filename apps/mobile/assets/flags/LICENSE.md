# Flag vector license

The simplified geometric flag vectors in `index.ts` were created for Second
Brain from public flag specifications. To the extent copyright can apply to
these original vector constructions, they are dedicated to the public domain
under **CC0 1.0 Universal**.

License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Country flags and their official designs can also be subject to national laws
on use and representation. The assets are decorative language markers; the
adjacent native language name remains the accessible source of meaning.
